# Interview Ready Guide

## What recruiters look for
- Clear communication and problem-solving structure.
- Practical examples from real projects and data pipelines.
- Strong understanding of SQL, Python, Azure, Power BI, and ETL.

## Strong portfolio message
- Show end-to-end thinking from data collection to insight delivery.
- Highlight automation, dashboards, optimization, and business impact.
- Keep your notes structured, visual, and easy to scan.

## Suggested talking points
1. Explain one project from requirement to deployment.
2. Describe how you handled data quality and performance.
3. Show how your portfolio reflects real engineering maturity.
